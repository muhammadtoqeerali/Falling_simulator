ARCHIVE CONTENT POLICY

PAPER_BUNDLE
============
Contains:
- final metrics
- pilot metrics
- confusion matrices
- training histories
- campaign logs
- configurations
- source-code snapshots
- CNN / normalizer source
- methodology notes
- label-count audit
- software/hardware metadata
- SHA256 manifest

Does NOT contain:
- model checkpoints
- giant X.npy feature caches
- raw datasets


REPRO_BUNDLE
============
Contains everything useful from PAPER_BUNDLE plus:
- best_model.pth checkpoints
- y.npy label cache where available

Does NOT contain:
- giant X.npy feature cache
- raw KFall / UniVRFall datasets
- raw Extended35 campaign data

Those datasets remain in their original storage locations and can be
regenerated/reloaded using the archived source code.
